# Product Vision — DahaMiz

---

## Vision Statement

DahaMiz is a global mobile-first platform where citizens can report environmental violations and pollution through
photos and videos.

We connect community participation with verified real-world environmental impact.

---

## Initial Market: Türkiye

We begin in Turkey with a culturally appropriate, privacy-respecting experience that meets Turkish legal requirements
and serves Turkish communities.

The product defaults to Turkish and uses a warm, neighborhood-oriented brand voice.

---

## Long-term Ambition: Global

The architecture, i18n infrastructure, and data model are designed for international expansion from day one.

---

## What Citizens Can Report

- Littering in streets and public spaces
- Cigarette butt littering
- Illegal waste dumping
- Construction debris dumping
- Waste discarded from vehicles
- Overflowing garbage and environmental hazards
- Water and air pollution
- Other environmental problems

---

## How It Works

1. Citizen observes an environmental problem
2. Opens DahaMiz and captures or selects media
3. Classifies the incident type
4. Confirms location
5. Reviews privacy information and consents
6. Submits to the secure reporting channel
7. Report is reviewed by DahaMiz moderators
8. If appropriate, forwarded to authorized public authorities
9. Authorities independently investigate using lawful procedures
10. Reporter receives appropriate status updates (legally appropriate scope only)

---

## What the Platform is NOT

- Not a government complaint form
- Not an automated enforcement system
- Not a surveillance or identification tool
- Not a public shaming platform
- Not a vigilante justice system

---

## Two Sides of the Product

### 1. Civic Reporting (Private, Serious)

Secure, private submission of evidence to the appropriate authorities. This side of the product emphasizes trust,
privacy, and seriousness.

### 2. Community Experience (Public, Engaging)

A premium consumer-quality social experience:

- Environmental stories and transformation posts
- Interactive activity map
- Challenges and missions
- XP, achievements, and leaderboards
- Personal impact profiles
- Community competitions

This side emphasizes engagement, positivity, and community identity.

---

## Core Values

- **Environmental responsibility** — every report is a real action with potential real impact
- **Community** — we are stronger together; local pride matters
- **Trust** — transparent, honest about what we can and cannot do
- **Privacy** — protecting reporters and third parties is non-negotiable
- **Legal integrity** — we support institutions, not replace them; AI is advisory only
- **Warmth** — this feels like a neighborhood product, not a government portal

---

## Brand Character — Fatih Abi

Our mascot and friendly guide is inspired by a warm, sincere neighborhood sanitation worker.

He represents trust, community, encouragement, and practical environmental wisdom.

He appears in onboarding, successful submissions, milestones, empty states, and tips.

He is not a joke character. He respects the dignity of sanitation workers.

See `docs/ASSET_REQUIREMENTS.md` for full character specifications.

## Competitive Positioning

This is NOT a generic government complaint form.

It should feel like an exceptionally well-designed consumer mobile application:

- Engaging
- Beautiful
- Polished
- Trustworthy
- Meaningful

We compete for attention with social apps — the quality bar is consumer product quality, not civic tech quality.

---

## Privacy as a Core Value

The platform processes potentially sensitive media containing identifiable third parties.

We take the following positions:

- Privacy by design, not privacy as an afterthought
- Original evidence is never publicly accessible
- Human review before any public publication
- No facial recognition or automated offender identification
- AI findings are advisory — never automatic legal grounds
- KVKK compliance is an architectural constraint, not a checkbox
